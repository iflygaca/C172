## Maintaining the references

1. Recheck the official listing/history rather than assuming a reachable old PDF or page is the current edition.
2. Record the exact GACAR section and PDF version, or AIP section, ICAO identifier, amendment and effective date for each claim.
3. Retain existing source hashes, hosted provenance, review status and rights restrictions until the content is actually revalidated.
4. Do not use FAA section numbering as a substitute for GACAR section numbering.
5. When a source changes, review affected English and Arabic content and generated/native copies separately.
